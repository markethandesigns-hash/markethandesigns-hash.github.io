What I can help with

    Once we know the plan, I stay with it and put it in place. Not a one-hour dump of advice and then you’re on your own.

  • Several AIs, and how to use them

    I can teach you how to use more than one AI. They don’t do the same job. One writes, one sorts a mess, one drafts a picture or a layout. We practice on your actual work, so you leave knowing which to open, and when a simpler way is better.

    Different bots for different things, and a straight answer on which ones are worth setting up.

  • Dashboards

Videos that keep people watching

Good video editing runs on loops. You open one early to get someone curious, and when it closes, most people click away. Keep another loop open and they stay with you. I can help you cut your videos that way.

  • Opening loops

    A hook in the first few seconds. A question, a before shot, a “wait until you see this.” It gives the viewer a reason to stay for the answer.

  • Closing loops, and the next one

    Pay off what you promised so it feels worth it. Then, before they leave, open a new thread: a next step, a teaser, a question you’ll answer in the next video. That’s what keeps them watching and coming back.
